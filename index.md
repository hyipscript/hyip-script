## The thing most buyers get wrong

They shop on the front end.

Every vendor posts the same investor dashboard with a price chart on it, so every script
looks identical and the whole decision turns into a design contest. But the front end is
the cheap half. You are the operator. You will spend your entire life in the admin panel
and about four minutes a month looking at the investor side.

So ask for admin screenshots before you pay anything. If a listing has ten front end
images and one sentence about the admin, that is a demo, not a product. A vendor who will
not show you the panel normally does not have much of one to show.

If you want something to compare against, the
[admin panel walkthrough](https://investmentscript.com/features/admin-panel/) is every
control laid out with screenshots.
