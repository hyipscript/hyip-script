# What a HYIP script costs

Prices are all over the place because the word "script" covers three different products.
Once you separate them the numbers make sense.

## The three price bands

**Under about 60 dollars.** Marketplace listings, zip file, no install, no support. You are
buying files. If you can deploy a PHP app and read a config file yourself this is real
money saved. If you cannot, you will spend more hiring someone to finish it than you saved.

**Roughly 150 to 500 dollars.** Installed and configured for you, working on your domain,
with a support window. This is where most first platforms land. The gap inside that band is
mostly design work and how many assets or currencies you need.

**Above 500 dollars.** Custom. You are paying for features that do not exist yet, or a
front end built to your spec rather than rebranded from a reference design.
[investmentscript.com](https://investmentscript.com/) quotes custom work as a fixed price
from the requirements, without a discovery call.

## What the price does not include

Domain and server. Budget maybe 10 to 15 dollars for the domain and 5 to 20 a month for a
VPS that will hold a starting platform. SSL is free. Anyone charging you for SSL is
charging you for Let's Encrypt.

Payment processing is separate too, and it is the part people forget. Manual crypto
deposits cost nothing and need no approval from anybody. A card gateway will want a
company, a bank account and an underwriting review, and for this business model most of
them will say no. Plan for crypto first.

## Subscription or one payment

One payment for the build. If a renewal exists it should buy you more support months and
nothing else. The moment a renewal can switch your platform off, you do not own it, you are
leasing it, and the price you were quoted was not the price.

## Where the money actually goes

Cheap scripts are cheap because the admin panel is thin. That is the expensive part to
build and the part nobody screenshots, so it is the first thing a low price cuts. Read
[admin panel controls that matter](admin-panel-controls.md) and check the listing against
it. That comparison explains most of the price differences you will see.
