# Crypto investment platform script

Same software, different label. A HYIP script, a crypto investment platform and an
investment script are the same category, and vendors pick whichever word their buyers
search for. Do not read anything into the name.

## What the crypto part changes

Deposits arrive on chain instead of through a card processor, so nobody has to approve you
first. That is the real reason this model runs on crypto. There is no underwriting, no
chargebacks, and no processor deciding in month three that they do not like your business.

The tradeoff is that crediting a deposit needs proof. Either you watch an address for
incoming transactions, or the user submits the transaction ID with a screenshot and you
approve it by hand. Manual approval is slower and it is also the version you want at the
start, because you see every payment before it becomes a balance.

## Bitcoin only, or more

Bitcoin alone is fine for a first platform. Adding USDT is usually the first request you
get, because people hate sending a volatile asset into a fixed return plan. Multi asset
support is normally the difference between a base package and the next one up.

## Withdrawals

Address field, queue, approval. Do not automate payouts on day one even if the script can.
Automated withdrawal with a bug is a very expensive bug, and you will not notice it until
the money is gone.

## The other platform types

The same core, plans and balances and payouts, gets rebuilt as gold, forex, real estate,
savings, staking and fixed deposit platforms. The maths and the admin panel barely change.
[investmentscript.com](https://investmentscript.com/) builds those variants from the same
base, so if your niche is not crypto the software still applies.

## What it is not

This is operator software. You run the platform, you hold the balances, you decide what
gets paid. If you are looking for somewhere to invest your own money, this is the wrong
side of the product entirely.
